Privacy Policy – Text & PDF Reader

Last updated: October 2026

Text & PDF Reader ("the app") is a reading, writing, and game app for Android. This policy explains how the app handles information.

No data collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or identifiers. We do not operate any servers that receive information from the app.

Local storage only

Books, PDFs, EPUBs, written content, and settings you add are stored only on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device.

Permissions

The app may request internet access, which is used only to load certain reading libraries when needed. It is not used to send any of your data anywhere.

No accounts, ads, or third parties

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, and does not share data with any third party, because it collects none.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this same page.
